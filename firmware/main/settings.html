01 / AUDIO & DISPLAY

LIVE keeps the microphone open during playback. Speak at any time to interrupt.

Saving a different voice clears context and starts a new session in the current mode. Saving the same voice keeps the session. Ask the assistant directly to change language, pace or response length.

Idle timeout applies to the next session. Device volume changes save automatically. Volume 0 is mute.

02 / CONTROLS

HOME: UP/DOWN selects VOICE, LIVE or SETTINGS. Tap OK to enter. Hold OK for 0.6 seconds to return. Settings return one level at a time; voice screens return home.

VOICE: tap OK to record, tap again to send. During a response, tap OK to stop. LIVE: speak hands-free, including during playback. Tap OK to stop a reply.

UP/DOWN adjusts volume by 5 in voice screens; hold to repeat. Returning home discards unsent input and pauses the microphone and playback. Context is retained while the session remains active. Reset context requires confirmation. The power button controls hardware power.

03 / FIRMWARE

Update on the device: SETTINGS > DEVICE > Firmware update > Check / install latest. The device checks, downloads, verifies and installs the published firmware, then restarts. Keep power connected.

04 / NETWORK

Save up to 8 networks. Connect your phone to the device setup hotspot to scan nearby Wi-Fi and check the password before saving. Your other saved networks are kept.

The device will display a JOIN HOTSPOT QR code. Scan it with your phone camera and join mic-on. The setup page should open; if it does not, use the second code shown automatically on the device.
